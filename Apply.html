Our mission

Protect the gastropods and insects that keep our world spinning.

The Bug Observation Project runs a digital headquarters where agents log findings in the field, even with no signal. Employees keep that network running.

Observe

Log sightings with a GPS pin, a time and a species. Every record goes to the BOP Command Center.

Protect

Move snails off roads, mark nesting sites and report habitat damage before it spreads.

Connect

Work with field partners on the global dispatch channel and share what you find with the rest of the agents.

FAQ

Before you apply

Do I need to be an expert on snails or insects?

No. Field Agents are trained on the job. Specialist roles list the skills they need, and curiosity counts for a lot.

Can I work remotely?

Most roles combine remote work with local patrols near you. HQ roles can be done fully from home.

How long until I hear back?

The recruitment office aims to reply within 10 days of receiving your application.

I made a mistake in my application. What now?

Submit a new one and mention the earlier application number in the story section. HQ will use the latest.

Where does my information go?

Only to the BOP recruitment office, and only to process your application.
